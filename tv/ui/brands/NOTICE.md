# Brand marks

These SVGs come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0 1.0). They are only used in the
browser demo of LocalHub TV, to stand in for apps that aren't installed; on a TV every tile shows the
app's own icon. The marks themselves are trademarks of their owners, and using them here doesn't mean
those companies endorse LocalHub.

`chromium.svg` and `browser.svg` are LocalHub's own simple drawings (MIT, like the rest of LocalHub), for the TV's two
streamed browsers.
