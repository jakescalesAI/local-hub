Your look

Colours, fonts, wallpaper and how widgets look, all in one panel.

Open it

Right-click empty space on Home and choose Edit Style…, press Edit Style while editing Home or find it in search. Changes show as you make them. Reset goes back to the start, and Done closes the panel.

You can also ask the assistant in plain words, and say “undo that” if you don’t like it.

What you can change

Theme
Apply a theme, export yours or import a theme file.
Appearance
Auto, Light or Dark.
Text and Spacing
Font: System, Rounded, Serif or Mono. Spacing: Compact, Regular or Roomy.
Accent
The colour of selected things.
Widgets
Solid, Glass or Outline, with sliders for blur, transparency and corners, and a choice of shadow.
Tab bar
Solid or Glass.
Effects
None, Grain or Glow.
Wallpaper
A built-in one, a gradient you make from 2 or 3 colours or a picture of your own.

Your own wallpaper

Upload a PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or AVIF of up to 10 MB. You can keep up to 40.

Save it or bring it back

Your Settings at the bottom of the panel has Undo, Export and Import.

  • Export saves your settings, Home, pages and your files, with no keys. Wallpapers you uploaded stay out.
  • Import takes a file of up to 40 MB and replaces your tabs, Home, pages, files and style. LocalHub asks first.
