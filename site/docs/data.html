Your data

No account to make. Your links, chats and settings are files on your PC.

What lives where

The desktop app keeps everything in one folder. To open it, press Windows R and enter %APPDATA%\LocalHub\hub. Three folders in there hold what’s yours.

config
Your settings and keys. Keep this private.
data
Home, chats, calendar, links and snippets, and the Browser’s sign-ins in data\browser.
workspace
What the assistant writes, and what you attach in chat, in workspace\uploads.

Installing a newer version leaves these three alone. The desktop app’s log is %APPDATA%\LocalHub\logs\hub.log.

What leaves your PC

Nothing is stored on a LocalHub server, because there isn’t one. These go out when you use them.

  • Chat: what you ask, and what the assistant reads to answer it, goes to Anthropic through Claude Code.
  • Browser and web search: the sites you and the assistant visit.
  • Icons and calendars: LocalHub asks saved sites for their icons and fetches any calendar link you added.
  • Optional keys: the AI, Stripe and Cloud tabs read from those services with your keys.
  • Other devices: your traffic travels over your own Tailscale network.

Back up

Your look and Home
Open Style and use Export under Your Settings.
Links and snippets
Use Export links and snippets in search.
Everything
Quit LocalHub from the tray and copy the three folders above. You can leave out data\browser. Keep the copy private, because config holds your keys.

Uninstall

  1. Quit LocalHub from the tray.
  2. Open Windows Settings, then Apps, find LocalHub and choose Uninstall.
  3. Your settings, keys and data stay on the computer. To remove them too, delete the %APPDATA%\LocalHub folder.

Claude Code, Tailscale and Chrome are separate programs. Uninstall them on their own if you want them gone.
