Privacy and terms

LocalHub has no server and no account, and collects nothing about you. It runs on your PC and keeps what’s yours there.

What LocalHub collects

Nothing. There is no LocalHub server, so there is nowhere to send it. The app has no analytics, crash reports or tracking, and it doesn’t check in with anyone when it starts. This site has no trackers, analytics or cookies either.

Where your data lives

Everything stays in %APPDATA%\LocalHub on your PC: your settings and keys in hub\config, your Home, chats, calendar, links and the Browser’s sign-ins in hub\data, and what the assistant writes in hub\workspace. Your data says how to back it up and remove it.

Your keys

The optional keys for the AI, Stripe and Cloud tabs are saved only in hub\config\secrets.json on your PC. LocalHub sends each key only to the service it belongs to, to read your own numbers there. It never sends a key anywhere else, never shows it to the assistant, and never puts it in a page.

LocalHub never reads, stores or passes on your Claude sign-in. Claude Code keeps that itself.

What goes to other companies

Only what you use, sent straight from your PC to them, under their own terms.

  • Anthropic: the chat runs Claude Code, Anthropic’s program, signed in with your own Claude account or API key. What you ask, and what the assistant reads to answer it, goes to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your account and Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Tailscale: if you reach LocalHub from your other devices, Tailscale knows which of your devices are on your network. Your traffic between them is encrypted end to end.
  • The sites you use: the sites you and the assistant open in the Browser or search, the icons of sites you save, and any calendar link you add.
  • Weather: if you add the Weather widget, LocalHub asks ipapi.co once for your rough location (it sees your public IP address) and gets the forecast from Open-Meteo.
  • Services you connect: Stripe, your cloud accounts and other AI providers, with the keys you gave them.
  • GitHub: the installer and new versions download from LocalHub’s GitHub releases.
