LocalHub docs

LocalHub is your own home screen, search bar and assistant. It runs on your Windows PC, and there’s no account to make.

Getting startedInstall it and go through first-run setup. Search and the launcherOne bar for links, snippets, files, tabs and actions. The assistantWhat it needs, what it can do and when it asks first. TroubleshootingWhat a message means and what to do about it.

What’s in it

Home and widgets
Your apps, sites and widgets, on pages you arrange.
Search and the launcher
Search everything from one bar, even when LocalHub’s window isn’t open.
Links and snippets
Web links, folders, Obsidian notes and any text you retype.
Files
Browse, search, rename and move the files on your PC.
Calendar
Your own events, plus a calendar you read from elsewhere.
The assistant
Ask in plain words, from any tab.
Browser
Websites in a pop-up, where you and the assistant can both work.
Your look
Themes, colours, wallpapers and how widgets look.

What it needs

LocalHub’s desktop app is for Windows only for now. A few things rely on other products, and LocalHub doesn’t install them for you. You get each one from its own site, and only when you want what it’s for.

Claude Code
For the assistant. Needs a paid Claude plan or an Anthropic Console account.
Tailscale
To reach LocalHub from your other devices.
Google Chrome
For the Browser pop-up.

Everything else works without them.
